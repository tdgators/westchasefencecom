Budget

What Drives the Price

Three things decide most of the cost of a fence in northwest Tampa.

Material & Height

Six-foot vinyl privacy costs more per foot than four-foot aluminum; composite and custom colors cost more still.

Length & Layout

Total linear footage and the number of corners and changes in direction.

Gates & Extras

Each gate, pool hardware, and removing an existing fence.
